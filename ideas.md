## Approach 1: "Cinematic Dark Universe" — Neo-Noir Streaming Platform

**Design Movement:** Neo-Noir Cyberpunk meets Premium Streaming (Netflix × IMAX)

**Core Principles:**
- Deep obsidian backgrounds with electric crimson/gold accents (not purple — avoid AI slop)
- Every section is a "show" or "episode" with its own card, trailer hover, and cinematic reveal
- Typography hierarchy: Display serif for titles, monospace for tech data, sans for body
- Motion as storytelling: parallax, cinematic fades, spotlight reveals

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: #0A0A0A (near-black obsidian)
- Primary accent: #E50914 (Netflix-inspired crimson, but used sparingly as a power color)
- Gold highlight: #D4AF37 (premium, award-winning feel)
- Text: #F5F5F1 (warm white, not cold)
- Muted: #6B6B6B

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-width hero with cinematic letterbox bars (top/bottom black bars)
- Horizontal scrolling "rows" for each career section (like Netflix rows)
- Cards with hover-expand "trailer" preview showing details
- Sticky left sidebar navigation (not top nav)

**Signature Elements:**
- "Now Playing" animated indicator on current section
- Card hover: scale up + glow + reveal overlay with stats
- "Runtime" badges showing years of experience like movie duration

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover on any card → cinematic zoom + info overlay
- Click → full-screen "episode" detail view
- Scroll → parallax depth layers

**Animation:**
- Entrance: cards slide in from bottom with stagger delay
- Hover: smooth scale(1.08) + drop shadow bloom
- Section transitions: horizontal slide like changing channels

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display (serif, cinematic)
- Code/Data: JetBrains Mono
- Body: DM Sans

## Approach 2: "Obsidian Prime" — Ultra-Dark Premium Dashboard

**Design Movement:** Dark Luxury Editorial × Streaming Dashboard

**Core Principles:**
- Asymmetric layout with a dominant hero and cascading content rows
- Glassmorphism cards with deep blur and subtle border glow
- Rich amber/gold accent system against near-black backgrounds
- Data-forward: skill meters, timeline bars, animated counters

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: #080C14 (deep navy-black)
- Accent: #F59E0B (amber gold — premium, warm, distinct from Netflix red)
- Secondary: #3B82F6 (electric blue for tech highlights)
- Glass: rgba(255,255,255,0.05) with backdrop-blur
- Text: #E2E8F0

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Left: Fixed cinematic sidebar with profile and nav
- Right: Scrollable content with Netflix-style horizontal rows
- Hero: Full-bleed video-style banner with animated text
- Each "row" = a career category (Experience, Skills, Education, Achievements)

**Signature Elements:**
- Animated skill bars that fill on scroll
- "Featured" spotlight card (current role at Meta) with cinematic glow
- Floating particle constellation background

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Cards expand on hover with smooth height animation
- "Add to Watchlist" → Download Resume button
- Rating stars showing proficiency levels

**Animation:**
- Particle background: slow-drifting constellation nodes
- Counter animations: numbers count up on scroll
- Row entrance: staggered card slide-in from right

**Typography System:**
- Display: Bebas Neue (bold, cinematic)
- Subheadings: Outfit (modern, clean)
- Body: Inter (readable)

## Approach 3: "Stellar Black" — Cosmic Premium Streaming Experience ✅ CHOSEN

**Design Movement:** Space-Age Luxury Streaming × Editorial Dark Mode

**Core Principles:**
- Cosmic depth: layered star fields, nebula gradients, orbital motion
- Every career milestone = a "featured title" with its own cinematic card
- Ultra-premium feel: subtle grain texture, micro-animations, bespoke typography
- Beyond Netflix: AI spotlight, interactive skill constellation, career timeline scrubber

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: #050810 (deep space black-blue)
- Primary: #C8A96E (warm champagne gold — premium, not corporate)
- Accent: #4F8EF7 (stellar blue for tech highlights)
- Nebula: rgba(79,142,247,0.15) soft glow overlays
- Text: #F0EDE8 (warm cream)
- Muted: #8892A4 (cool slate)

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-screen hero with animated star field + cinematic profile reveal
- Netflix-style horizontal scrollable rows per category
- Sticky top navigation with frosted glass effect
- "Featured Spotlight" section for Meta role (like Netflix's hero banner)
- Interactive career timeline as a horizontal scrubber

**Signature Elements:**
- Animated star/particle background that responds to mouse movement
- Cards with holographic shimmer on hover
- "Experience Meter" — glowing orbital rings showing years per company
- "Skill Constellation" — interactive node graph of skills

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Mouse parallax on hero section
- Card hover: holographic tilt effect + glow bloom
- Smooth section transitions with cinematic wipe
- "Play" button on hero → animated career story scroll

**Animation:**
- Star field: slow parallax drift
- Hero text: cinematic stagger reveal (letter by letter)
- Cards: 3D tilt on hover using CSS perspective
- Skill bars: orbital fill animation on scroll

**Typography System:**
- Display: Cormorant Garamond (elegant, editorial serif)
- Headings: Space Grotesk (modern, techy)
- Body: Manrope (clean, highly readable)
- Code: JetBrains Mono
